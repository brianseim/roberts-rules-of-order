## Bardic Performance

**30 rounds/day** — 4 base + 7 Cha + 12 (Extra Performance ×2) + 7 FCB

|Performance|Description|
|---|---|
|**Diversion (Su)**|Will DC 20 or target hears ghost sounds; Fitz may immediately Stealth to vanish. Replaces Distraction.|
|**Obfuscate (Sp)**|Grants invisibility (as spell) to self or creature within 30 ft. for as long as performance maintained. Lingering Performance extends 2 rounds after stopping. Replaces Fascinate.|
|**Piercing Crescendo (Su)**|Standard action. Ranged touch, 30 ft., no AoO. 1d4 piercing + 3 sonic. Ignores hardness and DR. Fort or Will DC 20 vs. Daze (1 round). Not subject to SR. Replaces Countersong.|
|**Shrouded Step (Sp)**|Greater Invisibility via performance (standard action, 1 BP/round). Persists through attacks. After successful Diversion, substitutes for Stealth as move action + 1 extra BP. Replaces Suggestion.|
|**Inspire Competence +3**|Ally within 30 ft. gets +3 competence bonus on one skill. Requires line of sight/hearing.|

### Sneak Attack

**+3d6** (1d6 base + 1d6 at L5 + 1d6 Accomplished Sneak Attacker)

- **Free** with Piercing Crescendo when target is flat-footed, invisible, after successful Diversion, etc.
- **+1 BP round** to apply when not naturally qualifying — no check or save needed.

## Background

**Family**

| | |
|---|---|
|**Father**|Corvin Tanglebram — fisherman, owner of the _Stellar Sturgeon_|
|**Mother**|Maesel Tanglebram — practical, quietly supportive|
|**Sibling 1**|Pip (Pipplewhistle) Tanglebram — age ~11, hero-worships Fitz|
|**Sibling 2**|Nessa Tanglebram — age ~8, disturbingly perceptive|

**Personality** Storyteller at heart. Collects tales from traveling bards. Performs for neighborhood kids. Eternally curious — asks questions to hear more, then recites the answers back to check he got them right. A blend of Tasslehoff Burrfoot (irrepressible curiosity, light fingers), Mickey Mouse (boundless enthusiasm, chaotic energy), Bilbo Baggins (unexpected depth, love of home mixed with hunger for adventure), and Scanlon Shortholt from Critical Role (charming performer, quick wit, always spinning a situation to his advantage).

**Goal** Get out of Sandpoint and see the world. Magnimar. Riddleport. Anywhere with stories worth telling.

**Fear** Spending his whole life on the _Stellar Sturgeon_.

**Deity** [[Desna]] — Goddess of Dreams, Stars, Travelers, and Luck (CG). Fitz lights a candle to her most nights, asking quietly when it will finally be his turn to go somewhere.

## Piercing Crescendo — Full Ruling Text

_(Robert Wilhelm ruling)_

Standard action. Ranged touch attack, 30 ft., no AoO. Deals 1d4 piercing + 3 sonic (½ level 7 = 3). Ignores hardness and DR. Note is inaudible to all but the target. Fort or Will save DC 20 (10 + 3 + 7 Cha) or dazed 1 round. Supernatural ability, not subject to spell resistance.

**Sneak Attack:** Free whenever Fitz naturally qualifies. Costs +1 BP round to force when he would not.

Range increases to 40 ft. at 8th level, 50 ft. at 15th.
