## Level 1 — Session Zero / Session 1

### Character Creation
- **Array:** Set Three (17, 16, 14, 12, 8, 7) + 5 bonus points (Core race)
- **Bonus point spend:** 7→8 Str (2 pts), 17→18 Cha (4 pts)
- **Racial:** +2 Con, +2 Cha, –2 Str applied
- **Final scores:** Str 5, Dex 16, Con 14, Int 14, Wis 8, Cha 20

### Stats
- HP: 10 (8 base + 2 Con)
- AC: 16 (10 + 2 leather armor + 3 Dex + 1 size)
- BAB: +0, CMB: –4, CMD: 10
- Fort +2 / Ref +5 / Will +1
- Initiative: +5 (Dex +3, Excitable +2)
- Speed: 20 ft.
- Bardic Performance: 16 rounds/day (4 base + 5 Cha + 6 Extra Performance + 1 FCB)

### Feats
- **Extra Performance** (level 1 feat) — +6 BP rounds/day
- **Lingering Performance** (drawback bonus feat) — BP effects persist 2 rounds after stopping

### Drawbacks
- **Naive** — –2 AC vs. improvised weapons; –2 CMD vs. dirty tricks
- **Forgetful** — DC 10 Wis check when leaving location or lose mundane item

### Traits
- **Excitable (Gnome)** — +2 Initiative
- **Naturally Gifted (Gnome)** — +1 extra gnome SLA use/day

### Skills (8 ranks)
Bluff 1, Diplomacy 1, Disable Device 1, Knowledge (Local) 1, Perception 1, Perform (Oratory) 1, Sleight of Hand 1, Stealth 1

### Spells Known
- 0 (4): Detect Magic, Prestidigitation, Mage Hand, Dancing Lights
- 1st (2): Cure Light Wounds, Jitterbugs

### Equipment
- Leather Armor (10 gp, 15 lb.) — later replaced
- Rapier (20 gp, 2 lb.)
- Scarf, bladed (12 gp, 2 lb.) — flavor only, no proficiency
- Ink, Portrait book, Inkpen, Belt pouch
- Starting gold: 105 gp (Bard avg 3d6×10)
- Remaining: 43 gp 9 sp (after equipment; daggers and shield removed session 1)

### Robert's Rulings Established
- Full bard spellcasting (not diminished)
- Piercing Crescendo gets Fort or Will save vs. daze (DC 10 + ½ level + Cha)
- Piercing Crescendo ignores hardness AND damage reduction
- Sneak attack FREE when naturally qualifying; costs +1 BP round to force
- Gnome FCB (+1 BP round/level) applies to Songfilch
- Songfilch casts in light armor without spell failure

### Class Features Gained
- Bardic Knowledge (+1)
- Bardic Performance (Diversion, Obfuscate, Piercing Crescendo)
- Sneak Attack +1d6
- Cantrips
